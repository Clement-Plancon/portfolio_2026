Public site (art direction « Affiches »):
- Archivo (archivo.woff2): from Google Fonts (variable, wght 100-900, wdth 62-125), subset with fontTools
  to Latin-1, œ/Œ, French punctuation (– — ‘ ’ “ ” „ … • and thin spaces) and €. The only typeface of
  the site: its width axis carries the design (wide capitals for titles, condensed figures, titles that
  stretch as they come in). It has no arrows: the site draws them in SVG. Also used by
  scripts/generate-og.mjs.

Administration only: Newsreader and Instrument Sans (Fontsource, Latin-1).
