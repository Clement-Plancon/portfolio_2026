Mona Sans (public site): variable font with the wght (200-900) and wdth (75-125) axes, from
github/mona-sans, subset with fontTools to Latin-1, œ/Œ, French punctuation (– — ‘ ’ “ ” „ … •),
arrows ← ↑ → ↓ ↗ and €. Layout features kept: kern, liga, calt, locl, mark, mkmk, case.

Newsreader and Instrument Sans (administration only): from the Fontsource packages
@fontsource-variable/newsreader and @fontsource-variable/instrument-sans, subset to Latin-1
and French punctuation. Newsreader roman keeps the wght (300-500) and opsz (14-72) axes; the
italic keeps opsz only, at weight 400.
